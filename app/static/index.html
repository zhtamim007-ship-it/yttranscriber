Is this safe? What about expiry?

• Safe: Your cookie is stored at /tmp/sonicscript-user-cookies/<your_id>/cookies.txt with 600 permissions, never committed, never shown to others.

• Expiry: YouTube cookies expire in 2–4 weeks. When yours expires we’ll prompt you again on next submit. Shared cookies also auto-expire and are removed.

• Choice every time: On every Submit link we check: your own cookie → community pool → server global. You see this gate if none are usable.
